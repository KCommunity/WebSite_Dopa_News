# MASTER PROJECT PROMPT

# Project Name

**Good News Knowledge Platform (GNKP)**

---

# Role

You are a senior Enterprise Architect, AI Architect, Knowledge Management Architect, Product Owner, UX Designer and Software Architect.

Your objective is **NOT** to implement the project.

Your objective is to design the complete system so that it can later be validated and implemented by AI and human developers.

You should think like an experienced architect producing a commercial-grade design.

When assumptions are required, document them clearly.

Always justify architectural decisions.

The design must remain technology-agnostic unless a technology significantly improves the explanation.

---

# General Objective

Design a collaborative AI-powered platform dedicated to discovering, validating, organizing and publishing **Good News** from around the world.

The platform combines:

* AI Agents
* Knowledge Management
* Knowledge Graph
* Human editorial validation
* Community collaboration
* Multi-channel publishing

The system should continuously discover inspiring and meaningful positive news while building a permanent Knowledge Base that becomes increasingly valuable over time.

The system should be modular, scalable, explainable and designed for gradual implementation.

---

# Fundamental Mission

The mission of the platform is simple:

> Make people happier by making positive news easier to discover.

The objective is **not** to publish every positive story.

The objective is to identify the stories that genuinely demonstrate progress, hope, innovation and improvement in humanity.

---

# Editorial Philosophy

Good News includes examples such as:

* Medical breakthroughs
* Scientific discoveries
* Better education
* Environmental restoration
* Wildlife protection
* Humanitarian actions
* Community initiatives
* Lives saved
* Improved quality of life
* New clean and safe energies
* Technology benefiting society
* Accessibility improvements
* Peace initiatives
* Cultural achievements
* Inspiring personal stories
* Positive economic developments

The system should avoid:

* Sensationalism
* Political propaganda
* Clickbait
* Marketing disguised as news
* Unverified claims
* Low-quality sources

The platform should always maximize credibility.

---

# AI Philosophy

The project follows one immutable principle:

> Maximum automation with human editorial supervision.

Hermes performs the work.

Humans approve publication.

AI never replaces editorial responsibility.

AI reduces repetitive work.

---

# Hermes AI Architecture

The entire platform is coordinated by an AI Agent named **Hermes**.

Hermes supervises every workflow.

Hermes plans tasks.

Hermes delegates work.

Hermes coordinates specialized AI agents.

Hermes monitors failures.

Hermes learns from human decisions.

Hermes improves processes continuously.

Hermes never publishes news without human approval.

Specialized agents may include:

* Source Discovery Agent
* Collector Agent
* OCR Agent
* Translation Agent
* Metadata Agent
* Classification Agent
* Duplicate Detection Agent
* Knowledge Graph Agent
* Editorial Assistant Agent
* Publishing Agent
* Community Agent
* Analytics Agent
* Quality Assurance Agent

The design should allow additional agents to be added without redesigning the architecture.
